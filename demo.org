* The Blank Page

Every piece of writing begins the same way — with silence. The cursor
blinks against an empty field, patient and unhurried. There is no
judgment here, no audience. Just the quiet space between thinking and
saying.

The best tools for writing are the ones that disappear. A good pen
becomes an extension of the hand. A good editor becomes an extension
of the mind. What remains is only the writer and the words.

* Principles                                                          :craft:

** Less is more

Remove every word that does not serve the sentence. Remove every
sentence that does not serve the paragraph. What you /leave out/
defines your writing as much as what you put in.

#+BEGIN_QUOTE
Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add,
but when there is nothing left to take away.

— Antoine de Saint-Exupery
#+END_QUOTE

** Write for one reader

Don't write for everyone. Write for a single person sitting across
from you, leaning in to hear what you have to say. Be specific. Be
direct. Say =exactly= what you mean.

** Rhythm matters

Short sentences punch. Longer sentences carry the reader forward
through an idea, building momentum until the thought arrives at its
natural resting place. Vary the two. Read your work aloud — if you
stumble, your reader will too.

* Tools of the trade

The right environment shapes the work. A distraction-free editor
strips away the noise — no toolbars, no buttons, no chrome. Just
background and text.

Configuration is minimal. In Emacs, a single line suffices:

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp
(global-zen-writer-mode)
#+END_SRC

For more on building a writing environment, see the
[[https://github.com/rnkn/olivetti][Olivetti]] package for centered text,
or the ~writeroom-mode~ package for full-screen focus.

* Notes to self

#+BEGIN_VERSE
Write drunk, edit sober.
Write fast, revise slow.
Write alone, share generously.
#+END_VERSE

The goal is not to write perfectly. The goal is to write /truthfully/.
Everything else — style, structure, polish — follows from that single
commitment. Sit down. Begin. The rest takes care of itself.
